# 12. When It's Hard

> *This chapter is an outline. It will grow through practice.*

---

Not every session is insight and clarity. Some sessions are about sitting with what's uncomfortable.

---

## Where This Chapter Is Going

- Presence over performance — meeting someone where they are, not where the framework is
- When compass domains pull opposite directions
- When intentions can't all be active at once
- Care through honesty — comfort without truth isn't caring, challenge without care isn't honest
- The session where naming the tension IS the entire work
- Avoidance as information: what you keep not talking about
- When the practice itself becomes avoidance (staying in the workshop to avoid the world)
