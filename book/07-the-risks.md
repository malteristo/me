# 7. The Risks Nobody Talks About

> *This chapter is an outline. It will grow through practice.*

---

AI psychosis is apparently real. Dependency is real. The erosion of independent thinking is real. A practice that takes AI partnership seriously must also take the risks seriously.

---

- How the practice itself is the safeguard — structure prevents the worst failure modes
