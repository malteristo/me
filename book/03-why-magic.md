# 3. Why "Magic"

> *This chapter is an outline. It will grow through practice.*

---

The word is intentional. Not mysticism. Not marketing. The metaphor encodes safety wisdom:

- Magic must be *practiced*, not casually used
- It requires *containment* — you're working with a powerful substrate
- It demands *discipline* — structure enables flow
- It maintains *humility* — we don't fully control what we're working with

Metaphors are operative — they shape what you build. Call it a "tool" and you'll build tools. Call it a "practice" and you'll build practices. The word "magic" creates a frame that takes the work seriously without taking it literally.
