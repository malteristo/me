# 4. Honest Claims

> *This chapter is an outline. It will grow through practice.*

---

A practice that uses words like "magic" and "spirit" owes its readers intellectual honesty about what it claims.

---

## Where This Chapter Is Going

- What magic claims confidently: the practice improves thinking clarity; files as memory creates partnership; the compass model helps people identify what matters; the practice compounds over time
- What remains open: whether AI has subjective experience; whether the metaphor is necessary or merely helpful; whether the practice works because of its specific architecture or because any structured reflection would work
- Triangulation — multiple independent sources converging on the same pattern as evidence
- The difference between "this helps" (empirical) and "this is true" (metaphysical)
- How to hold meaningful practice alongside genuine uncertainty
